L.A.M.P. – Modular Lever Arm Assist

Engineered a modular retrofit attachment for material handling equipment using SolidWorks and 3D prototyping to increase mechanical leverage. Modeled the assembly as a cantilever beam to verify bending moments, safely sustaining operational loads up to 150 lbs with a hybrid sand-cast A356 Aluminum and steel tube construction.
